### 📥 1. Admin Executive PDF Roadmap Export (100% English Landscape A4)
To help us present player feedback directly to game producers and developers:
- Admins now have an **`📥 Export PDF (EN)`** engine in **Game Evolutions**.
- **1 Proposal Per Landscape Page**: Generates an executive briefing deck complete with priority tier gauges (*Critical, High Demand, Active Interest*), vote scores, player badges (Pseudo & Server #), and full specifications.
- **Smart Feedback Synthesis**: Summarizes community commentary into clear takeaways for the dev team.

### 🌐 2. One-Click Auto-Translation on Player Proposals & Comments
Language is no longer a barrier!
- Whenever someone posts a proposal or leaves a comment in French, Korean, Russian, Japanese, Spanish, German, or Chinese... you’ll see a clean **`🌐 Translate`** button.
- One tap translates the entire text into your active site language in real time.
- Switch back to the original wording anytime with **`Show original`**.
- **Zero-Lag Cache**: Translations are saved locally in your browser for instant 0ms reload!

### ⚡ 3. Universal Spotlight Search (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`)
- Hit **`Cmd + K`** (Mac) or **`Ctrl + K`** (Windows/Mobile) anywhere on the wiki.
- **Instant Search**: Type any hero, damage type (Kinetic / Beam / Ion), flagship deck, active gift code, or calculator to jump straight to it.
- **Filter Pills**: Quickly filter by *Champions, Guides, Flagships, Tools, Events, or Codes*.

### 🌐 4. Full 23-Language Support Across All Features
- All menus, tools, and the Spotlight Search adapt 100% to all 23 supported languages (`EN`, `FR`, `DE`, `ES`, `JA`, `KO`, `ZH`, `RU`, `PT`, `IT`, `PL`, `AR`, etc.).
