## 📢 [UPDATE] August 20, 2026 — Universal Spotlight Search (Cmd+K), Streamlined UI & 23 Languages!

Hey Commanders! 👋

We just pushed a massive quality-of-life update to **FGF WIKI** to make your daily gameplay, theorycrafting, and warzone prep faster and smoother than ever.

Here is everything new you can use right now:

---

### ⚡ 1. Universal Spotlight Search (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`)
No more clicking through menus to find that one hero skill or calculator!
- Hit **`Cmd + K`** (Mac) or **`Ctrl + K`** (Windows/Mobile search icon) anywhere on the site.
- **Instant Live Search**: Type any hero name, damage type (Kinetic / Beam / Ion), flagship deck, event, active gift code, or tool to jump straight to it in milliseconds.
- **Filter Pills**: Filter instantly by *Champions, Guides, Flagships, Tools, Events, or Codes*.
- **Full Keyboard Navigation**: Use `↑` / `↓` arrows and hit `Enter` to open.

### 🌐 2. 100% Search & UI Localization in 23 Languages
- The new Spotlight Search and all tool menus now fully adapt to all **23 supported languages** (`EN`, `FR`, `DE`, `ES`, `JA`, `KO`, `ZH`, `RU`, `PT`, `IT`, `PL`, `AR`, and more).
- Search using localized hero names or terms in your own language!

### 🧹 3. Cleaner, Faster Navigation
- Cleaned up redundant tabs to focus 100% on strategic guides, fleet meta decks, and calculators.
- Page load times are now virtually instant with optimized bundle packaging.

### ⭐ 4. Local Favorites System (Under the Hood)
- We've laid the groundwork for saving and pinning your favorite champions, deck setups, and guides directly in your browser.
